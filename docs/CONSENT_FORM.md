# Consent to Record — Ekegusii Speech Dataset

**Draft.** R3 owns this; have it checked against your institution's research-ethics requirements
before the first recording drive.

---

## Spoken version (read into the recording, saved as `consent_{speaker_id}.wav`)

> "My name is ______. Today is ______. I understand that this recording is being made by students at
> ______ to build a computer system that can understand and write down Ekegusii. I understand that my
> recording and a written version of what I say may be shared publicly so that other people can use it
> for research and for building Ekegusii language tools. My name will not be attached to it. I am
> giving this recording freely and I have not been paid for it. I can ask for my recording to be
> removed at any time by contacting ______. I agree."

## Written version

I, ____________________, agree to be recorded speaking Ekegusii for the Ekegusii Speech Recognition
project at ____________________.

I understand that:

1. My voice recording and a written transcription of it will be used to train speech-recognition
   software.
2. The recording and transcription may be published publicly as part of an open language dataset, so
   that others can build Ekegusii tools.
3. **My name will not be published.** I will be identified only by an anonymous code.
4. I am not being paid, and I am giving this voluntarily.
5. I may withdraw my recording at any time by contacting the project team at ______________________,
   and it will be removed from any future version of the dataset.

Signature: ______________________  Date: ______________

**Project contact:** ______________________

---

**For the team — non-negotiable:**
- No consent, no recording. No exceptions, no "we'll get it later".
- The name↔`speaker_id` mapping is kept **off the repo and off the group chat**. R3 holds it.
- If a speaker withdraws, R7 removes their clips and rebuilds the dataset version. Log it.
